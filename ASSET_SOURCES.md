# Asset sources

- Miniature units, defenses, countryside mesh, and props: original work generated for this repository by `Tools/Blender/generate_assets.py` and the Unity editor builders (`PrototypeBuilder`, `DioramaBuilder`, `DioramaSurface`, and `DioramaVegetation`). Editable Blender source is retained. No external model attribution is required.
- UI/outcome cues: original deterministic synthesis in `PrototypeBuilder.Tone`, exported as WAV. Combat sounds now use edited recordings, listed below.
- Liberation Sans / TextMeshPro resources: supplied with Unity's `com.unity.ugui` package. The imported font's license is retained alongside it in `Assets/TextMesh Pro/Fonts/`. Liberation Fonts use the SIL Open Font License; retain that license when distributing the font.
- Unity URP template, packages, and shaders: Unity-provided components, governed by their package license files.

No historical faction insignia or third-party reference art is included.

- Meadow/road pigment, wheel ruts, earth strata, grass clusters, and stones: original deterministic procedural assets. Their generator code is retained in the Unity editor tools. No external textures were used.

- Broadleaf tree and shrub meshes: original leaf/branch geometry generated in `Tools/Blender/generate_assets.py`, including individual leaf UVs. Leaf veins and sandbag weave: original procedural textures generated in `FoliageMaterials.cs`. No external foliage art or texture source.

- Revised farmhouse architecture and coursed slate roof: original procedural Blender geometry in `generate_assets.py`, with editable source retained in `miniatures.blend`. No external building assets.

- Field gates, raised checkpoint boom, road signs, fences, stakes, timber revetments, duckboards and field stones: original Blender field kit in `generate_assets.py`. Soil/root and stencil-sign textures: original deterministic editor generation in `DioramaSurface` and `BattlefieldDetails`.

- Gunshot recordings: Tabasco / Vincent Sevedge, [Gunshot Sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/gunshot-sounds). The archive specifies CC BY 3.0, which we follow despite CC0 on the page. Original copyright notice, sources, editing details and license link are retained under `Tools/Audio/`; attribution ships in `StreamingAssets/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt`.
- Material impacts: Kenney, [Impact Sounds 1.0](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds), CC0. Explosion body: Spring Spring, [Mechanical Explosion](https://opengameart.org/content/mechanical-explosion), CC0. Selected source files/licenses are retained under `Tools/Audio/Sources`. Short mono variations are generated by `CombatPresentationBuilder`; sources are trimmed, filtered, pitched, normalized, faded and layered.
- Flash, smoke, dust, spark and brass textures: original code-generated alpha stamps in `CombatPresentationBuilder`. Runtime emitters and projectiles are pooled. No external VFX package.

- Expanded enemy roster: original Blender scouts, assault infantry, transport trucks, armored cars and heavy tanks, with editable generation/source retained. Static surfaces combine by material; limb, carry and wheel hierarchies remain articulated. No external models or Kingdom Rush assets/code were imported.

## November 10 additions

Nine tower tier/branch models and the barn are original procedural Blender assets in `Tools/Blender/generate_assets.py` and `miniatures.blend`. The countryside ambience is original generated PCM; its repeatable source is `Tools/Audio/generate_ambience.py`, with no third-party samples. See `Tools/Audio/AMBIENCE.md`. The app icon is generated with the built-in imagegen tool; the selected project PNG and complete prompt are recorded in `Tools/Blender/APP_ICON.md`. No new external game assets or packages were added.

- Countryside refinement: original StoneCottage, VillageInn, Dairy, ToolShed and VillageWell geometry in `Tools/Blender/generate_assets.py`, with editable `miniatures.blend` and exported FBX/prefabs retained. Connected road pigment, yard clearings, grass tufts and scenery placement are original deterministic editor generation in `CountrysideLayout`, `PastureDetail` and `CountrysideSurroundings`. Existing barn/foliage/field-kit materials are reused. No external models, textures or packages were added.

## Winter Forest

The snow spruce, birch, signal hut, foxhole, wood sledge, outcrop and painted trail marker are original Blender models authored for this project. Regenerate their FBX files and editable scene with `Tools/Blender/winter_forest.py`. The snowfield pigment, placements and weather emitters are generated by the Winter Forest editor tools. The winter wind loop is original synthesized audio generated by `Tools/Audio/generate_winter_wind.py`.

## Title screen

The two preview photographs are HUD-free captures of the authored Unity battlefields. The bookcloth and stamped insignia are original graphics. The title wordmark is a raster rendering of DIN Condensed Bold. The menu labels use the project's bundled TextMesh Pro font.
